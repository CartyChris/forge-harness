## Works (tested on SwiftShader, tests/t_shaders.py → FAILS: none on --only 71,72,73 AND full build; t_wallpapers.py still FAILS: none on full build)
- plugins/73-shaders.js: 10 raw-WebGL fragment-shader backdrops `gl-aurora, gl-nebula, gl-chrome, gl-synth, gl-ocean, gl-tunnel, gl-crystal, gl-blackhole, gl-ink (cat 'calm'), gl-lattice`, group 'gpu'. - Service `forge.shaders`: ids, probe(id,{mouse,t}), compileErrors(), thumb, start, sampleLive(), current(), stats() (live context count, thumbContext).
- Gallery (71-backdrops, gallery-only edits): "GPU shaders" category; gpu tiles render a real frame via `b.thumb(cv)` on ONE shared offscreen GL context (programs cached per scene); 'gen' tab now excludes gpu.
